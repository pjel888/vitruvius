# `.ai` Operating System

This is the live planning and governance layer for Vitruvius.

Current product direction is KG-first/AEC-MEPFS-first and source-grounded AI-first: authored taxonomy/backbone work is the center of gravity, while raw, conversion, canonical, derived, and operational layers exist to attach real documents, evidence spans, provenance, source rights, embeddings, graph projections, and validation custody to that graph. Preserve raw-source immutability, source-rights/protected-payload gates, the distinction between source-backed facts and admitted graph reasoning, and production graph/KG mutation gates when reconciling any live planning document.
