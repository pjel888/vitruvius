## Purpose

The previous Architecture branch had useful L2/L3 coverage but its L4 children were mostly scaffolding labels such as `Concepts`, `Concept Anchor`, and `Rule Context`. This research pass converts the Architecture branch from placeholder anchors into real navigable topics that can support Explorer browsing and resolver grounding.

## Normalized Architecture topic families

The Architecture branch now needs real semantic children under existing L3 paths, especially:

- World and regional architecture chronology: prehistoric, ancient/classical, medieval/sacred, Renaissance, Baroque, industrial, modern, contemporary, Southeast Asian and Philippine traditions.
- Theory and design schools: Vitruvian principles, classical proportion, modernist doctrines, phenomenology/place theory, critical regionalism, parametric/systems thinking.
- Professional practice: scope of practice, architect of record, signing/sealing, responsible control, contracts, procurement, ethics, liability, project/office management.
- Laws/codes/compliance: occupancy, construction type, height/area, egress, zoning, permits, overlays, accessibility, fire authority and agency clearances.
- Design process and studio: problem framing, parti, massing, schematic design, critique response, design decision logs, precedent translation.
- Building technology: systems coordination, shafts, risers, grids, envelope interfaces, construction systems, commissioning access.
- Materials/envelope/details: concrete, masonry, steel/timber/composite, finishes, waterproofing, rainscreen, air/vapor/thermal control layers, movement joints.
- Environmental systems: daylighting, ventilation, thermal comfort, acoustics, indoor environmental quality, tropical climate response.
- Urban/site/housing/accessibility/heritage/interiors/digital/documentation/administration: real topic labels instead of generic anchors.
