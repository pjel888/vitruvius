# Research — AEC Incidental Remediation Backlog

## Purpose
Provide a structured place to park incidental out-of-scope fixes discovered during bounded first-slice execution so they are not lost and do not silently widen the sprint.

## Conclusion
The contingency protocol is stronger if every deferred adjacent/unrelated issue has one bounded backlog entry with:
- file/path
- issue class
- why it surfaced
- why it was deferred
- expected blast radius
- recommended follow-on trigger
