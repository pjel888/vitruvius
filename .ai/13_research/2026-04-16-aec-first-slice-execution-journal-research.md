# Research — AEC First-Slice Execution Journal

## Purpose
Define a compact execution journal format for the future bounded first-slice implementation so implementation notes, incidental issues, and closure evidence stay readable and reviewable.

## Conclusion
The future implementation phase should keep one bounded journal entry structure that records:
- what was attempted
- what changed
- what incidental issues appeared
- what regressions were run
- what remains blocked

This improves execution traceability but does not close G10.
