# Research — AEC Readiness Vocabulary

## Purpose
Freeze a single vocabulary for AEC readiness/state language so control docs stop drifting between “planning-only”, “implementation-ready”, “blocked”, and “evidence-pending”.

## Conclusion
The bounded first-slice corpus should use these terms distinctly:
- planning-gate ready
- baseline-ready
- evidence-pending
- execution-ready
- historical checkpoint

This removes ambiguity between clean-baseline truth and actual execution-evidence truth.
