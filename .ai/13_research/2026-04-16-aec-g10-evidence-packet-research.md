# Research — AEC G10 Evidence Packet

## Purpose
Freeze the exact evidence packet the future implementation session should collect when attempting to close G10.

## Conclusion
A future G10-closure attempt should not rely on scattered terminal scrollback. It should populate one compact evidence packet covering:
- baseline identity
- test-file presence
- fixture-file presence
- command outputs
- artifact-root inspection
- final closure verdict

This improves later closure discipline but does not close G10.
