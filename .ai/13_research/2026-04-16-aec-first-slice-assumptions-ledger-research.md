# Research — AEC First-Slice Assumptions Ledger

## Purpose
Freeze the assumptions the bounded first-slice planning corpus depends on, and define what kinds of evidence would invalidate them before or during execution.

## Conclusion
The handoff is now detailed enough that assumption drift is a real risk. A bounded assumptions/invalidation ledger is justified so future execution can distinguish:
- assumptions still holding
- assumptions weakened by new evidence
- assumptions invalidated and requiring replanning
