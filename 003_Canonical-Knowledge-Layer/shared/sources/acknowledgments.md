## Key Points

- The Technical Working Group (TWG) on Disaster Preparedness of the Department of Education (DepED) gratefully acknowledges the invaluable help attributed to several personnel of the DepED family in the realization of this publication.
- The Philippines is considered one of the most disaster-prone countries.
- Luzon with estimated cost at about PHP 3.1 billion and affected about 8 million school children in both elementary and secondary schools.
