- SECTION 13.16 REGULATIONS IN BUFFER/GREENBELT ZONE (B/G-Z)These are yards, parks or open spaces intended to separate incompatible elementsor uses to control pollution/nuisance and for identifying and defining developmentareas or zones where no permanent structures are allowed.

## Key Points

- ▪ Open spaces/gardens/landscapes - ▪ Parks and park structures such as playgrounds, jogging trails, bicycle lanes - ▪ Plant nurseries - ▪ Ground-level or underground parking structures/facilities - ▪ Agriculture, silviculture, horticulture - ▪ Customary accessory uses incidental to any of the above .
