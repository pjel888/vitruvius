## Overview

SUBJECT : Statement that Architectural (and Architectural Interior) Design are Exclusive Parts of the Scope of the Practice of Architecture as Defined Under R.A. No. 9266 (The Architecture Act of 2004) and its 2004 Implementing Rules and Regulations
