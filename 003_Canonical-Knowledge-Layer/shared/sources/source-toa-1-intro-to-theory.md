## Key Points

- Definition of Theory and Architecture Abstract thought or speculation resulting in a system of assumption or principles used in analyzing, explaining, or predicting phenomena, and proposed or followed as a basis of action.
- Architectural Systems and Orders - ▪ Organizational pattern, relationships, hierarchy - ▪ Qualities of shape, color, texture, scale, proportion - ▪ Qualities of surfaces, edges, and openings - ▪ Approach and entry - ▪ Path configuration and access - ▪ Sequence of spaces - ▪ Light, view, touch, heari.
- Processes in Architectural Design A purposeful activity aimed at devising a plan for changing an existing situation into a future preferred state.
