## Space

Space is the three-dimensional field in which objects and events occur and have relative position and direction, especially a portion of that field set apart in a given instance or for a particular purpose.

![image 8](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile8.png>)

###### Spatial Relationships

Spaces may be related to each other in several fundamental ways:

- ▪ Space within a space;
- ▪ Interlocking spaces;
- ▪ Adjacent spaces; and
- ▪ Spaces linked by a common space.

![image 9](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile9.png>)

## Proxemics

Proxemics is the study of the symbolic and communicative role of the spatial separation individuals maintain in various social and interpersonal situations, and how the nature and degree of this spatial arrangement relates to environmental and cultural factors.

![image 101](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile101.png>)

###### Personal Space

The variable and subjective distance at which one person feels comfortable talking to another. Also called personal distance.

| |
|---|

![image 102](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile102.png>)

###### LEVELS OF DISTANCES

![image 103](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile103.png>)

- ▪ Intimate Distance
- ▪ Personal Distance
- ▪ Social Distance
- ▪ Public Distance

![image 104](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile104.png>)

###### Intimate Distance

###### 0-18 inches (0-450mm).

![image 105](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile105.png>)

Voluntarily selected gap between people who are drawn to each other. At this close range, vision is distorted and any vocalization is a whisper, moan, or grunt.

![image 106](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile106.png>)

![image 107](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile107.png>)

###### Personal Distance

###### 18 inches to 4 feet (450mm-1.20m)

The sense of body heat is lost. Eyesight begins to focus, and vocalization comes into play. Although only ritualized touch is typical, the other person is still at arm’ s length, available to be grasped, held, or shoved away.

![image 108](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile108.png>)

###### Social Distance

4 to 10 feet (1.20m - 3.00m)

![image 109](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile109.png>)

This is the zone of impersonal transaction. We now have to rely solely on what we can see and hear.

By the middle of the range, the eye can focus on an entire face. When the distance is more than eight feet, it’s OK to ignore another’s presence and it’s easy to disengage from a conversation.

![image 110](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile110.png>)

![image 111](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile111.png>)

###### Public Distance

10 feet to infinity (3 meters and beyond)

This is the zone where we can no longer pick up subtle nuances of meaning from the face or tone of voice. The eye can take in the whole body at a glance.

It’s the distance of the lecture hall, mass meetings, and interactions with powerful figures until such time as they bid you to come closer.

![image 112](<TOA 3 (Design Perception)_images/imageFile112.png>)
