Coulomb’s Law of Charges

##### CURRENT

- - designated by the symbol “I”
- - determined by the number of electrons that pass through a cross-section of a conductor in one second
- - unit of measurement is amperes, “A”

Charles A. Coulomb discovered that charged bodies attract or repel each other with a force that is directly proportional to the product of the charges, and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them
