# UAP Document 301 General Conditions

## Overview

The Architect, in performance of his services to his Client, prepares the “General Conditions” as one of the Contract Documents. The General Conditions define the relationship among the Owner, the Architects and the contractor and stipulates the norms by which the contractor shall perform his work.

- SECTION 1 DEFINITIONS AND DOCUMENTS
- SECTION II LAWS, REGULATIONS, SITE CONDITIONS, PERMITS AND TAXES
- SECTION III EQUIPMENT AND MATERIALS
- SECTION IV PREMISES AND TEMPORARY STRUCTURES
- SECTION V PROTECTION OF WORK AND PROPERTY
- SECTION VI LABOR, WORK AND PAYMENTS
- SECTION VII CONTRACTOR-SEPARATE CONTRACTORS-SUB-CONTRACTORSRELATIONS
