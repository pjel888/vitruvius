- Section 299 of the RA 7160 Local Government Code of the Philippines authorizes provinces, cities, and municipalities to issue bonds and other long-term securities, in accordance with the Central Bank and the Securities and Exchange Commission regulations. These financial instruments are intended to fund self-liquidating, income-producing development or livelihood projects aligned with approved local development plans or public investment programs. The issuance of these securities requires the approval of an ordinance by a majority of the Sanggunian members, which must clearly state the terms, conditions, and the specific purpose of the incurred debt.

The Implementing Agency must determine the minimum Performance Terms and Conditions (PT Cs) of the JV PPP contract, including any government veto rights.
