- • Used in Planning Temples
- • In concentric layout
- • Four major zones

- – Brahma Padas
- – Devika Padas
- – Manusha Padas
- – Paisachika Padas

###### • Padas: Foot

![image 10](<Hindu Architecture_images/imageFile10.png>)

###### – Brahma Padas

• where the main and/or largest idol of the temple resides

###### – Devika Padas

• the zone where gods (devas) reside

###### – Manusha Padas

• zone where human beings live and symbolically make choice between good and evil

###### – Paisachika Padas

• zone of demons, fears, doubts, suffering. On the east edge are always Surya (Sun) and Indra present, who rythmically bring light and hope everyday

###### • Nagara Architecture

- – Also known as the Northern Style
- – In plan, the temple is a square with a number of graduated projections in the middle of each side giving a cruciform shape with a number of reentrant angles on each side.
- – The projections in the plan are also carried upwards to the top of the Sikhara and, thus, there is strong emphasis on vertical lines in elevation.
