## Key Points

- Body of Water - – Riverbanks - – Lakes - – Seashores - – Artificial Pond(Areas without Water) - • Lush Landscaping - • Inside caves or carved stones similar to Chaityas (Rock-cut Temples in Buddhism) - • Hilltops and Mountain Slopes with Serene Vista - • Inside Forests.
- Used in Planning Temples - • In concentric layout - • Four major zones - – Brahma Padas - – Devika Padas - – Manusha Padas - – Paisachika Padas • where the main and/or largest idol of the temple resides • the zone where gods (devas) reside • zone where human beings live and symbolically make choice .
