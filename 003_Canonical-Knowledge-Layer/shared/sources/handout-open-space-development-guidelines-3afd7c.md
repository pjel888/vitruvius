## Coverage

- 1. Preserve: A development approach that aims to maintain and protect existing natural or cultural features thathave significant value in open spaces. It involves keeping these areas untouched to prevent any form of degradation.Possible open spaces that may apply preservation strategies include old-growth forests, wetlands, or open spaces thatare marked as important cultural properties.
- 2. Conserve: A development approach that involves the careful management of natural and cultural resources toprevent further degradation, destruction, or neglect. While almost similar to preservation, conservation allows a levelof use or public access higher than areas of preservation, and may include management strategies that provide slightregeneration to minimize harm or alteration of the open spaces as natural resources.
- 3. Regenerate: A development approach that involves the restoration of areas that have been degraded, damaged,or destroyed. Strategies may involve activities like replanting native vegetation, rehabilitating wildlife habitats, orrestoring the functionality of ecosystems and its services. These strategies are also more likely to be applied to non-blueand non-green open spaces.
- 4. Create: This development approach involves the creation of entirely new open spaces, often in urban environmentsand/or in non-blue and non-green open spaces. This could involve transforming open spaces into community gardens,new parks, or creating green roofs and walls on buildings to minimize non-grey surfaces and increase permeability toimprove hydrologic systems.
