# Water Quality Management Area

## Definition

A water quality management area is a designated physiographic area such as a watershed, river basin, or water resources region used for coordinated water quality governance.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-philippine-clean-water-act-of-2004](law-philippine-clean-water-act-of-2004)** - Section section-5: "The Department shall designate certain areas as water quality management areas using appropriate physiographic units such as watershed, river basins or water resources regions."
