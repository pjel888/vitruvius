# Competitive Bidding

## Definition

Competitive bidding is a procurement method open to participation by any interested party under the Government Procurement Reform Act.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-government-procurement-reform-act](law-government-procurement-reform-act)** - Article article-iii-e: "Competitive Bidding refers to a method of procurement which is open to participation by any interested party."
