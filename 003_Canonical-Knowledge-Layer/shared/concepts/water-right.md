# Water Right

## Definition

A water right is the government-granted privilege to appropriate and use water under the Water Code.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-water-code-of-the-philippines](law-water-code-of-the-philippines)** - Article article-13: "Water right is the privilege granted by the government to appropriate and use water."
