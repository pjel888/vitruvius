# Built Heritage

## Definition

Built heritage refers to architectural and engineering structures, their settings, and landscapes with notable historical and cultural significance.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-national-cultural-heritage-act-of-2009](law-national-cultural-heritage-act-of-2009)** - Section section-3-f: "Built heritage" shall refer to architectural and engineering structures such as, but not limited to, bridges, government buildings, houses of ancestry, traditional dwellings, quartels, train stations, lighthouses, small ports, educational, technological and industrial complexes, and their settings, and landscapes with notable historical and cultural significance.
