# Local Autonomy

## Definition

Local autonomy is the genuine and meaningful authority of local government units to govern, develop, and administer their jurisdictions under the Local Government Code.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-local-government-code-of-1991](law-local-government-code-of-1991)** - Section section-2-a: "The territorial and political subdivisions of the State shall enjoy genuine and meaningful local autonomy."
