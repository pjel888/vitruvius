# Comprehensive Land Use Plan

## Definition

A comprehensive land use plan is the primary and dominant zoning-based plan that guides the future use of land resources within a local government unit.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-local-government-code-of-1991](law-local-government-code-of-1991)** - Section section-20-c: "The local government units shall continue to prepare their respective comprehensive land use plans enacted through zoning ordinances which shall be the primary and dominant bases for the future use of land resources."
