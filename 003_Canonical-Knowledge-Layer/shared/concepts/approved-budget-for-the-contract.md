# Approved Budget for the Contract

## Definition

The Approved Budget for the Contract is the budget for a contract duly approved by the head of the procuring entity.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-government-procurement-reform-act](law-government-procurement-reform-act)** - Article article-iii-b: "Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC) refers to the budget for the contract duly approved by the Head of the Procuring Entity."
