# Waste Disposal

## Definition

Waste Disposal is the requirement that liquid and solid wastes from health facilities be discharged, collected, treated, and disposed of in accordance with applicable codes and ordinances.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[handout-doh-guidelines-for-health-facilities](handout-doh-guidelines-for-health-facilities)** - Section section-waste-disposal: "Waste Disposal: Liquid waste shall be discharged into an approved public sewerage system whenever available, and solid waste shall be collected, treated and disposed of in accordance with applicable codes, laws or ordinances."
