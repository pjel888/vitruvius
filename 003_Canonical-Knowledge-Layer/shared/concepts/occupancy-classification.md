# Occupancy Classification

## Definition

- (a) Buildings proposed for construction shall be identified according to their use or the character of its occupancy and shall be classified as follows: - (1) Group A – Residential Dwellings - Group A Occupancies shall be dwellings. (2) Group B – Residentials, Hotels and Apartments - Group B Occupancies shall be multiple dwelling units including boarding or lodging houses, hotels, apartment buildings, row houses, convents, monasteries and other similar building each of which accommodates more
