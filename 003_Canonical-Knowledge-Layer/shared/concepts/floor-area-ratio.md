# Floor Area Ratio

## Definition

Floor Area Ratio is the ratio between the gross floor area of a building and the area of the lot on which it stands.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-zoning-ordinance-of-the-municipality-of-san-mateo](law-zoning-ordinance-of-the-municipality-of-san-mateo)** - Section section-definition-of-terms: "Floor Area Ratio (FAR): The ratio between the Gross Floor Area of a building and the area of the lot on which it stands; determined by dividing the Gross Floor Area of the building and the area of the lot."
