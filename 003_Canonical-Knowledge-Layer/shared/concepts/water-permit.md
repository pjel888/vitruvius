# Water Permit

## Definition

A water permit is the document evidencing the government-granted water right required for appropriation or regulated groundwater drilling under the Water Code framework.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-water-code-of-the-philippines](law-water-code-of-the-philippines)** - Article article-13: "No person shall appropriate water without a water right, which shall be evidenced by a document known as a water permit."
- **[irr-amended-implementing-rules-and-regulations-of-the-water-code-of-the-philippines](irr-amended-implementing-rules-and-regulations-of-the-water-code-of-the-philippines)** - Section section-45: "No person shall drill any well for the extraction of groundwater or make any alteration to any existing well without securing a permit from the Board."
