# Patient Movement

## Definition

Patient Movement is the requirement that circulation spaces in health facilities support safe and unobstructed movement of patients, beds, stretchers, and wheelchairs.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[handout-doh-guidelines-for-health-facilities](handout-doh-guidelines-for-health-facilities)** - Section section-patient-movement: "Patient Movement: Spaces shall be wide enough for free movement of patients, whether they are on beds, stretchers, or wheelchairs."
