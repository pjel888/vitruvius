# Bids and Awards Committee

## Definition

The Bids and Awards Committee is the committee established under the Government Procurement Reform Act to manage procurement processes.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-government-procurement-reform-act](law-government-procurement-reform-act)** - Article article-iii-c: "BAC refers to the Bids and Awards Committee established in accordance with this Act."
