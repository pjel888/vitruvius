# Wastewater Charge System

## Definition

The wastewater charge system is the fee-based regulatory system for discharging wastewater into water bodies within designated management areas.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-philippine-clean-water-act-of-2004](law-philippine-clean-water-act-of-2004)** - Section section-13: "The Department shall implement a wastewater charge system in all management areas through the collection of wastewater charges/fees."
