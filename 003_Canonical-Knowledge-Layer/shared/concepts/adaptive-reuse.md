# Adaptive Reuse

## Definition

Adaptive reuse is the utilization of buildings, built structures, and sites of value for purposes other than their original use in order to conserve the site and its engineering integrity and authenticity of design.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-national-cultural-heritage-act-of-2009](law-national-cultural-heritage-act-of-2009)** - Section section-3-a: "Adaptive reuse" shall refer to the utilization of buildings, other built-structures and sites of value for purposes other than that for which they were intended originally, in order to conserve the site, their engineering integrity and authenticity of design.
