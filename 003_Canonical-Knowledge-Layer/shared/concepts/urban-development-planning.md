# Urban Development Planning

## Definition

Urban development planning is the planning process for diverse elements of an urbanizing or urbanized area, including infrastructure, environment, housing, transportation, land use, and urban growth.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-department-of-human-settlements-and-urban-development-act](law-department-of-human-settlements-and-urban-development-act)** - Section section-3-i: "Urban Development Planning refers to the process that involves the planning of diverse elements that comprise an urbanizing and urbanized area, including its physical infrastructure, environment, housing, transportation and management of land use and urban growth."
