# Designated Establishment

## Definition

A designated establishment is a private or public entity identified by the DOE as an energy-intensive industry or establishment.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-energy-efficiency-and-conservation-act](law-energy-efficiency-and-conservation-act)** - Section section-4-c: "Designated establishment refers to a private or public entity identified by the DOE as an energy intensive industry or establishment."
