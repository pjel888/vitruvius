# Discharge Permit

## Definition

A discharge permit is the legal authorization granted by the Department for the discharge of wastewater into a water body under the Clean Water Act.

## Regulatory Basis

- **[law-philippine-clean-water-act-of-2004](law-philippine-clean-water-act-of-2004)** - Section section-14: "The discharge permit shall be the legal authorization granted by the Department to discharge wastewater."
