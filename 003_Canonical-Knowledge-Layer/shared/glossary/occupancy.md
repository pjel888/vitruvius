# Occupancy

## Definition

Occupancy refers to the purpose for which a building or a portion of a building is used or intended to be used.

## Classification Context

In the context of the National Building Code of the Philippines, occupancy determines how a structure is categorized under occupancy classifications, which in turn define applicable regulatory requirements.

## Key Implications

- Determines applicable building code provisions  
- Influences fire safety and life safety requirements  
- Affects allowable density and occupancy load  
- Impacts design requirements such as exits, ventilation, and sanitation  

## Related Concepts

- occupancy classification  
- occupancy load  
- means of egress  
- fire-resistive construction  

## Notes

The term “occupancy” must always be interpreted within the context of the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), where specific definitions and classifications are formally established.

Misinterpretation of occupancy can result in incorrect classification and non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
