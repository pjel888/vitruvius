# Occupancy Classification Governs Fire-Resistive Requirements

## Relationship Summary

Occupancy classification determines the level of fire-resistive requirements that must be applied to a building or structure.

## Explanation

Under the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of the National Building Code of the Philippines, different occupancy groups present different levels of risk to life and property.

As a result, each occupancy classification is assigned corresponding fire-resistive requirements, including:

- type of construction
- fire separation distances
- allowable materials
- fire protection systems

Higher-risk occupancies, such as assembly or institutional uses, typically require stricter fire-resistive measures compared to lower-risk occupancies.

## Implications

- Incorrect occupancy classification leads to incorrect fire safety design  
- Fire-resistive construction cannot be determined independently of occupancy  
- Compliance review must evaluate both occupancy and fire provisions together
