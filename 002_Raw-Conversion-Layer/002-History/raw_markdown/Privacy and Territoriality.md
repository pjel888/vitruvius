- PRIVACY
- • the ability of an individual or groups of individuals to control their visual, auditory, olfactory interactions with others
- • the ability to have options and to achieve desired level of interactions

## KINDS OF PRIVACY

Solitude: state of being free from

observation by others Intimacy: state of being with another person

but free from the outside world

Anonymity: state of being unknown even in a crowd

Reserve: state in which a person employs

psychological barriers to control unwanted intrusions
