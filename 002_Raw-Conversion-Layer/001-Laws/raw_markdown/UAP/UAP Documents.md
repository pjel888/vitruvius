## SUSPENSION OF WORK

Contractor can suspend work:

- 1. non- payment after 15 days request
- 2. order of government to stop work for 90 days


Owner can suspend work:

- 1. bankruptcy- declared by BIR
- 2. insubordination
- 3. non payment of contractors debt
- 4. non superintendence of contractor
